## 🧠 What I Learned

The review helped me understand that not every piece of feedback needs to be implemented immediately.

I prioritized changes that directly affected:

- What I do
- How clearly my work is presented
- Whether my skills are believable
- The main action I want visitors to take
- Any confusing or broken parts of the portfolio

I focused on fixing the highest-impact issues first instead of trying to change everything at once.
